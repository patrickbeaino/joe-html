Who We Are

Driven by

Entracte Creative Agency crafts bespoke promotional assets — trailers, teasers, promoreels — meticulously designed to capture the unique identity of each film.

Driven by a passion for cinematic storytelling, we explore the visual and emotional core of every project to create a compelling narrative experience.

By combining this artistic focus with deep international expertise, Entracte helps producers, distributors, and sales agents elevate their films and resonate authentically across global territories.
